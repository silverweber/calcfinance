How Our Calculations Work

All of our calculators use the standard EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) formula used by banks and financial institutions worldwide:

EMI = P × r(1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ – 1)

Where P is the principal loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate divided by 12), and n is the number of monthly payments. This is the reducing balance method — the same formula used by major banks globally.
