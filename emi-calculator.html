What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment — a fixed payment amount a borrower makes to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. EMIs are used to pay off both interest and principal each month so that over a specified number of years, the loan is fully paid off.

EMI-based loans are the most common form of consumer lending in India, the UK, the US, and globally. Banks, NBFCs, and credit unions all use this structure for home, personal, and vehicle loans.

EMI Formula Explained

The standard EMI formula uses reducing balance method:

EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N – 1]

Where P = Principal loan amount, R = Monthly interest rate (Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), N = Loan duration in months. Each month, the outstanding principal reduces, so the interest component shrinks while the principal component grows.

Ways to Reduce Your EMI Burden

  • Negotiate a lower rate — check multiple banks and compare offers actively.
  • Make a higher down payment to reduce the principal and thus the EMI.
  • Extend the loan tenure — this lowers EMI but increases total interest paid.
  • Part-prepay the loan — reduce outstanding principal to lower future EMIs.
  • Balance transfer — move your loan to a lender offering a lower interest rate.
  • Maintain CIBIL score above 750 to access the best rates from banks.
