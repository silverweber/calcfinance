Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CalcFinance ("we", "us", or "our") operating at calcfinance.online collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

CalcFinance is a free calculator tool. We do not require you to create an account or submit any personal information to use the calculators.

The only data that may be collected is:

  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent, browser type, device type, and approximate location (country/city level) collected automatically via Google Analytics.
  • Cookies — small text files placed on your device by Google Analytics and Google AdSense for analytics and advertising purposes.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it
  • Display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics. Google Analytics uses cookies to report on website interactions. The information generated by the cookie about your use of this website is transmitted to and stored by Google.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Google AdSense & Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our site. Google AdSense may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

For more information about how Google uses data from sites that use its services, visit: Google Privacy & Terms.

5. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use the following types of cookies:

  • Analytics cookies — set by Google Analytics to track site usage
  • Advertising cookies — set by Google AdSense to display relevant ads

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some website features.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

CalcFinance is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
